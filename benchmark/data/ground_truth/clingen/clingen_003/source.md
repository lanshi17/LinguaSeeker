# Erratum in: Inner Retina Changes in ABCA4 -Related Retinopathy: Structure–Function Relationship

Erratum in: “Inner Retina Changes in ABCA4 -Related Retinopathy: Structure–Function Relationship” by Andrea Maria Coppé, Giancarlo Iarossi, Alessandro Cappelli, Giorgio Placidi, Sergio Petroni, Paola Valente, Antonio Novelli, Paolo Maltese, Matteo Bertelli, Luca Buzzonetti, and Benedetto Falsini ( Invest Ophthalmol Vis Sci . 2025;66(14):55), https://doi.org/10.1167/iovs.66.14.55 .

When the article was first published, there was an error in one of the author affiliations, and a funding source was inadvertently left out. In the article online, the first author affiliation has now been changed to Unit of Ophthalmology, Bambino Gesù Children's Hospital, IRCCS, Palidoro (Rome), Italy, and the funding section in the Acknowledgments has been changed to “Supported by Mediolanum bank grant to G.I. and B.F. and by the Italian Ministry of Health with ‘Current Research funds.’”
