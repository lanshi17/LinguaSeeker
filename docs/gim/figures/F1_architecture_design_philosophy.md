# Quiet Systems

Quiet Systems treats a complex pipeline as a sequence of calm, legible transformations. Form is modular and geometric: softly rounded containers, precise alignment, and thin directional lines establish a clear reading order without visual noise. Generous white space separates the main system from its evaluation protocol, allowing the architecture to be understood before the quantitative comparison is read.

Color functions as a restrained information code. Muted blue identifies acquisition and English-language processing, warm apricot identifies translated-language processing, pale mint marks normalization and validated outputs, and soft lavender denotes human review. Saturation remains low throughout. The palette should feel meticulously calibrated by an expert rather than decorative, with charcoal typography and cool gray connectors providing a stable visual foundation.

The rhythm moves from broad intake to a focused dual-track core, then converges toward standardized evidence and expert review. The dual-track split and merge form the visual center of gravity. Scale communicates importance: the extraction module is slightly larger than surrounding stages, while small repeated document and evidence tokens show multiplicity without requiring explanatory prose.

Typography is sparse, clinical, and consistently aligned. Labels are short nouns or compact noun phrases; supporting terms appear as small metadata rather than sentences. Simple line icons clarify module identity, but every icon remains subordinate to the data flow. The composition must appear painstakingly refined, with exact spacing, optical alignment, and no overlapping labels.

The lower evaluation band mirrors the split above through two parallel experimental paths. Shared inputs and matched conditions are visible spatially, while the outcome metrics sit at the convergence point. This visual echo is subtle but essential: it links system mechanism to experimental evidence without adding a separate explanation. The final artifact should show master-level execution, as if every curve, gap, label, and color value had been adjusted through many rounds of careful review.
