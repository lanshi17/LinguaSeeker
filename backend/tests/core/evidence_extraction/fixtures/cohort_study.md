# Cohort Study: ABCA3 Variant Frequency in Neonatal Respiratory Failure

## Study Design

A multi-center case-control study was conducted across 12 neonatal intensive care units. Cases were infants with unexplained neonatal respiratory failure. Controls were term neonates without respiratory symptoms.

## Cohort Size

The study enrolled 450 cases and 1,200 controls. Cases were identified through the Neonatal Respiratory Failure Registry. Controls were matched for gestational age and birth weight.

## Case Definition

Cases required: (1) respiratory distress within 24 hours of birth, (2) oxygen requirement >48 hours, (3) exclusion of known causes (infection, cardiac malformation). Genetic testing was performed using a targeted gene panel.

## Variant Frequency

ABCA3 variants were identified in 34/450 cases (7.6%) vs 12/1,200 controls (1.0%). The most common variant was c.1882G>A, found in 8 cases and 2 controls.

## Statistical Analysis

The odds ratio for any ABCA3 variant was 8.2 (95% CI: 4.2-16.0, p<0.0001). For c.1882G>A specifically, OR=11.3 (95% CI: 2.4-53.2, p=0.002). Analysis used logistic regression adjusted for population stratification.

## Population Frequency

In gnomAD, c.1882G>A has an allele frequency of 0.0004 (European non-Finnish). The variant is absent from the homozygote database, consistent with severe recessive disease.

## Detection Methodology

Cases and controls underwent identical sequencing protocols. Variant calling used GATK HaplotypeCaller with stringent quality filters. Detection methodology equivalence was confirmed by comparing coverage depth between groups.
