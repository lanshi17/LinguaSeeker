# Case Report: ABCA3 Deficiency in a Neonate

## Clinical Presentation

A term male neonate presented at 3 days of age with progressive respiratory distress and cyanosis. The proband was born to non-consanguineous parents of European descent. Family history was unremarkable for lung disease.

## Phenotype

The patient exhibited tachypnea, intercostal retractions, and oxygen desaturation. Chest imaging showed bilateral diffuse ground-glass opacities. Echocardiography revealed pulmonary hypertension. Biochemical markers demonstrated elevated lactate dehydrogenase.

## Genetic Testing

Whole exome sequencing identified a novel homozygous variant in the ABCA3 gene: c.1882G>A (p.Glu628Lys). Parental testing confirmed both carriers. Segregation analysis showed the variant segregated with disease in the family. De novo status was excluded by parental carrier confirmation.

## Diagnosis

The patient was diagnosed with ABCA3 deficiency based on clinical presentation, imaging findings, and genetic confirmation. Age of onset was neonatal. The mode of inheritance was autosomal recessive.

## Treatment

The patient received supportive care including mechanical ventilation and supplemental oxygen. The clinical course was consistent with severe neonatal respiratory failure.
