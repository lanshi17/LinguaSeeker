## Supplementary Note S3: Translation Quality Validation

Multi-stage translation pipeline safeguards (implemented in `backend/src/core/cross_lingual_translation/`):

1. **Terminology preservation:** translation prompts constrain the handling of gene symbols, HGVS strings, and disease names; terminology echo artifacts are stripped in post-processing.
2. **Structural alignment:** paragraph, table, and image-reference structure is preserved and validated between source and target.
3. **Draft translation:** initial LLM translation pass with context-window management (whole-document or segmented mode).
4. **Review & refinement:** post-processing passes correct punctuation/placeholder normalization and strip prompt/source-contamination artifacts.
5. **Validation:** deterministic checks verify translation completeness, per-block coverage, and target-language conformity, and flag redacted/missing values before the translated document enters extraction.

## Supplementary Note S4: Ablation Run Wall-Clock Durations

Per-entry pipeline wall-clock durations recorded by the ablation runner (`duration_s` in `en_only_metrics.json` / `dual_track_metrics.json`; n = 30 per mode). Runs executed against the live backend service under normal operating load; durations therefore reflect deployment conditions rather than a controlled latency benchmark.

| Mode | n | Mean (s) | Median (s) |
|------|---|----------|------------|
| EN-only | 30 | 759.6 | 623.1 |
| Dual-track (EN+ZH) | 30 | 612.6 | 416.5 |

The two modes were executed in separate batches at different times; between-batch differences in backend load dominate the mode difference, so these figures characterize practical throughput (~7–13 min per entry end-to-end), not the marginal cost of the second track.
