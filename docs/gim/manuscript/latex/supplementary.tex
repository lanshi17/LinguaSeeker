\section*{Supplementary Note S3: Translation Quality Validation}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Supplementary Note S3: Translation Quality
Validation}

Multi-stage translation pipeline safeguards (implemented in
\path{backend/src/core/cross_lingual_translation/}):

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Terminology preservation:} translation prompts constrain
    the handling of gene symbols, HGVS strings, and disease names;
    terminology echo artifacts are stripped in post-processing.
  \item \textbf{Structural alignment:} paragraph, table, and
    image-reference structure is preserved and validated between source
    and target.
  \item \textbf{Draft translation:} initial LLM translation pass with
    context-window management (whole-document or segmented mode).
  \item \textbf{Review \& refinement:} post-processing passes correct
    punctuation/placeholder normalization and strip
    prompt/source-contamination artifacts.
  \item \textbf{Validation:} deterministic checks verify translation
    completeness, per-block coverage, and target-language conformity, and
    flag redacted/missing values before the translated document enters
    extraction.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Supplementary Note S6: Frozen Stage-0 combining rule}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Supplementary Note S6: Frozen Stage-0
combining rule}

The original-language case series is Stage-0 analysis, not blinded
Stage-1 ACMG assignment. Product extraction continues to leave
\texttt{assigned\_acmg\_codes} empty.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Facts, not author codes.} The extractor recovers variant
    class, parental genotypes, parentage confirmation, diagnosis, and
    phasing. The rule reads those fields. It does not copy strings such
    as PS2+PM2+PP3 from the paper.
  \item \textbf{Granted codes.} PM6 requires a point variant, an affected
    proband, both parents tested and negative at the target, unconfirmed
    parentage, and inheritance that is not maternal or paternal (Moderate;
    never upgraded to Strong from a single report). PVS1 applies to
    nonsense or frameshift alleles with e2 protein position $\leq$ p.Glu472
    (Very Strong; distal truncations are PVS1\_Moderate and were not
    observed here). PP4 applies when the diagnosis is Rett syndrome or a
    compatible neurodevelopmental phenotype (Supporting). PM1 applies to
    missense residues in the methyl-CpG-binding domain (p.90--162) or the
    transcriptional repression domain hotspot (p.302--306). PS2 is never
    granted when parentage is unconfirmed. Population frequency (PM2) is
    not granted from historical paper annotations.
  \item \textbf{Combining.} Pathogenic combinations are tested first.
    One Very Strong plus one Moderate is Likely Pathogenic, not
    Pathogenic; adding one Supporting reaches Pathogenic. Missense
    alleles without PVS1 therefore often remain evidence-insufficient
    even after PM6 (for example, PM6+PP4, or PM1+PM6+PP4).
  \item \textbf{Conflicts.} ClinVar expert-panel Benign, maternal
    inheritance, and CNV or unmapped intervals block classification.
    rett\_081 is the on-disk maternal anti-example for T170M.
  \item \textbf{English-visible masking.} Without \texttt{A.variant\_hgvs\_c}
    in the English-visible layer, that layer is not scorable. After HGVS
    is present, variant class and VCEP residue stay on the allele.
    Parental and diagnosis fields are masked unless they appear in the
    English-visible layer.
  \item \textbf{Transcripts.} \textit{MECP2} events were mapped to the
    Rett VCEP e2 transcript \texttt{NM\_004992.3}. Paper HGVS on
    \texttt{NM\_001110792.2} (e1) was not treated as a separate allele.
  \item \textbf{Languages still missing.} Owned Japanese files
    (\texttt{rett\_055}, \texttt{rett\_062}, \texttt{rett\_063}) are a
    review or FOXG1/CDKL5 papers. Hashed \texttt{rett\_088} is a
    142-patient Japanese cohort (Kondo 2002); only three representative
    alleles were frozen, and cohort-level parental origin was not treated
    as named-proband PM6. Hashable German MECP2 point-variant case
    reports were not located (Deutscher 2002 is paywalled; a 2022 Rostock
    congress deck redacted the variant).
    A French source is a 2018 conference poster, not a journal article.
    Korean \texttt{rett\_067} is a 34-patient cohort without parental
    testing; only three representative alleles were frozen so the table
    is not inflated by every row of Table~2.
\end{enumerate}
