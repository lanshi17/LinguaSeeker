1. 引言

“急性缺血性卒中（AIS）是美国第四大死亡原因，通常以大脑某区域血流突然中断为特征，导致不可逆的脑损伤和神经功能损害”[1]。“卒中是美国的第五大死亡原因和致残的主要原因”[2]。“AIS由脑动脉的栓塞性或血栓性闭塞引起。栓塞性AIS是由于身体其他部位形成的血块阻塞脑动脉所致。而血栓性AIS则是由大脑内部形成的血块阻塞脑动脉引起，占所有急性缺血性卒中病例的50%以上”[1]。“研究表明，血脂异常是卒中的主要危险因素之一，这些因素包括血清或血浆总胆固醇（TC）和甘油三酯（TG）水平升高。低密度脂蛋白胆固醇（LDL-C）、载脂蛋白B（ApoB）水平升高，以及高密度脂蛋白胆固醇（HDL-C）水平降低，是发生急性缺血性卒中和动脉粥样硬化进展的高危因素”[3,4]。“这些血浆脂质参数可能受到环境因素的影响，如高血压、饮食、肥胖、人口统计学特征、运动、吸烟、饮酒以及遗传因素”[3,4]。

“肝脏从细胞合成的胆固醇或摄入的饮食中汇集胆固醇。血清或血浆低密度脂蛋白胆固醇（LDL-C）水平升高是动脉粥样硬化发生和进展的最重要危险因素之一，降低LDL-C水平仍然是血脂异常管理的主要目标”[5]。“肝脏是帮助维持胆固醇稳态的主要器官，低密度脂蛋白受体（LDL-R）被用作调控机制之一[6]。LDL受体在胆固醇稳态中起着重要作用，它调节肝脏对LDL颗粒的摄取，并将胆固醇输送到肾上腺和性腺用于合成类固醇激素，以及输送到肝脏用于合成胆汁酸”[7]。“LDL-R是一种跨膜糖蛋白，在载脂蛋白B介导的过程中，对从血液循环中摄取LDL-C起着重要作用”[8,9]。“LDL-R在中性pH条件下特异性地、高亲和力地与细胞外脂蛋白颗粒结合”[10]。“然后，LDL-C和LDL-R复合物通过内吞作用进入细胞”[11]。“LDL-C随后在酸性pH条件下从LDL-R上释放，被溶酶体降解，从而释放出游离胆固醇，并使LDL-R返回细胞表面”[10,12-14]。“人类LDL受体基因位于19号染色体上，包含18个外显子，由17个内含子分隔”[15]。“已报道的LDL-R基因突变超过770种”[16]。“据报道，LDL-R基因的突变会导致家族性高胆固醇血症”[17]。在本研究中，我探讨了LDL-R基因Ava II（rs5925）多态性对缺血性卒中患者血浆脂质参数的影响。

2.4 DNA Analysis

“Genomic DNA was isolated from blood leukocytes using DNA Qiagen kits according to manufacturer instructions. The extracted DNA was stored at $4^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ until analysis. The quality and quantity of extracted DNA were determined using spectrophotometric method with NANODROP 1000 ${ }^{\mathrm{R}}$ (Thermo Fisher Scientific, United States of America), which quantified the amount of extracted DNA in nanogram per microlitre $(\mathrm{ng} / \mu \mathrm{L})$ and assessed the quality (purity) based on the ratio of absorbance at $260 \mathrm{~nm}: 280 \mathrm{~nm}$ for

all the samples. The Genotyping of the LDL-R gene Ava II polymorphism was carried out using polymerase chain reaction and restriction fragment length polymorphism (PCR-RFLP) method described” by Long et al [18]. “PCR amplification was carried out with forward primer 5’ GTCATCTTCCTTGCTGCCTGTTTAG-3’ and reverse primer 5’-GTTTCCACAAGGAG GTTTCAAGGTT-3” [18]. Each amplification reaction was performed in a total volume of $25 \mu \mathrm{L}$, containing $3 \mu \mathrm{L}$ of genomic DNA, $1.0 \mu \mathrm{L}$ of each primer, $7.5 \mu \mathrm{L}$ of ddH $_{2} \mathrm{O}$, and $12.5 \mu \mathrm{L} 2 \times$ Taq PCR Master Mix (constituent: $0.1 \mathrm{U}$ Taq polymerase/$ \mu \mathrm{L}, 500 \mu \mathrm{M}$ dNTP each and PCR buffer). The PCR Cycling conditions for LDL-R gene SNP was: initial denaturation at $94^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ for 3 min, followed by 30 cycles of denaturation at $94^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ for 30s, annealing at $55^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ for 30s, and extension at $72^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ for 60 s, with a final extension at $72^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ for 5 min. After electrophoresis on a $2.0 \%$ agarose gel with $0.5 \mu \mathrm{g} / \mathrm{mL}$ ethidium bromide, the amplification products were visualized under ultraviolet light. The PCR aliquot $(8 \mu \mathrm{L})$ was digested with $10 \mathrm{U}$ of Ava II enzyme at $37^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$, followed by electrophoresis on $2 \%$ agarose gel stained with ethidium bromide, $0.5 \mathrm{mg} / \mathrm{ml}$ in Tris-borate EDTA. After restriction enzyme digestion of the amplified DNA, the genotypes were identified by electrophoresis on $2.0 \%$ agarose gels and visualized with ethidium-bromide staining ultraviolet illumination. The genotyping of these samples were completely consistent.

2.5 Statistical Analysis

Data were presented as Mean $\pm \mathrm{SD}$; GraphPad prism computer software version 5.01 was used to compare lipid profiles and lipoprotein ratios levels between genotypes for control and stroke subjects respectively. Allele frequencies were determined via direct counting and the standard-goodness-of-fit test was used to test the Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium. One-way ANOVA posthoc Turkey’s test was used for comparing significant differences between wild type and mutant genotypes for both separate subjects. A P-value $<0.05$ was considered statistically significant.

4. DISCUSSION

In this study, the polymorphism in the LDL receptor gene was determined by PCR-RFLP. The polymorphism in this study was presented in 81.05% (A+A+=72.63% and A+A+=8.42%) of patients with ischemic stroke with high lipid profile and 26.96% (A+A+=23.48% and A+A+=3.48%) of the non-symptomatic normolipidemic control subjects. The A+ allele was significantly (P<0.05) abundant in the stroke subjects compared with the control (44.74% vs. 15.22%) subjects, while the A- allele was significantly (P<0.05) higher in the control subjects compared with the stroke (84.78% vs. 55.26%). Different studies that are in line with this study have shown that the frequency of A+ allele was higher in phlegm-dampness constitution [19], acute myocardial infarction [20], and lower in patients with atherosclerotic cerebral infarction [21] when compared with healthy control subjects respectively. Salazar et al [22] found “that the frequency of A+ allele was significantly higher in 50 Familial hypercholesterolemia (FH) patients than in 130 healthy controls subjects (58% vs. 45%; P = 0.036). The FH patients had a higher frequency of A+A+ genotypes when compared to the normolipidemic controls (P = 0.0292) individuals”. In another study carried out by Salazar et al. [23] study shows that the frequency of A+A+ mutant genotype was higher in 170 white subjects presenting a lipid parameter with high

risk for Coronary heart disease (CHD) compared to that of the 130 controls individuals (32% vs. 16%) from São Paulo City in Brazil.

For the control subjects, the homozygous wild type (A+A) has significant (P<0.001) higher HDL-C and HDL-C/LDL-C values compared with the heterozygous (A+A+) and homozygous (A+A+) mutant. The subjects with A+A genotype also had significant (P=0.0001) lower plasma levels of TC, TG, VLDL-C, LDL-C, Non-HDL-C, TC/HDL-C, TG/HDL-C, LDL-C/HDL-C, AIP and AC than subjects with A+A+ and A+A+ genotypes. The association of LDL-R gene Ava II polymorphism and plasma LDL-C showed that there was a significant (P<0.0001) increase in LDL-C levels in A+A+ and A+A+ genotypes compared to A+A genotype for the normolipidemic subjects. Pongrapeeporn et al. [24] research work showed that the mean LDL-C levels were slightly higher in the A+A+ genotype than in the A+A+ and A+A genotypes in 54 normolipidemic Thai subjects. Humphries et al. [25] study showed that the individuals with one or two A alleles (absence of cutting site) had LDL-C levels that were lowered by 8.0% and 12.4%; respectively. Variation associated with this RFLP explained 4.2% of the sample variance.

携带A+A+和A+A+基因型的受试者，其血浆TG、TC、VLDL-C、TC/HDL-C、TG/HDL-C、LDL-C/HDL-C、AIP和AC水平显著高于携带A+A基因型的卒中受试者（P=0.0001-0.0063）。卒中患者的血浆TC、LDL-C和Non-HDL-C水平从A+A+基因型到A+A+基因型，再到A+A基因型显著下降（P<0.0001）。与A+A+基因型相比，A+A基因型的血浆HDL-C水平有显著增加（P=0.0005），而三种基因型间的HDL-C/LDL-C比值未显示出任何显著差异（p=0.1993）。我们观察到A+等位基因的纯合性与杂合性与缺血性卒中患者更严重的血脂异常存在关联。结果表明，A+A+和A+A+基因型以及A+等位基因是缺血性卒中的危险因素，而A+A基因型和A-等位基因是缺血性卒中的保护因素。“三种基因型之间LDL-R基因Ava II多态性与血浆脂质参数关联的差异，可能是由于不同的LDL-R基因Ava II多态性所致。一些研究未显示LDL-R基因Ava II多态性与血清或血浆脂质水平存在显著关联。
